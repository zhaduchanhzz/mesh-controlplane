- **Known limitations / security blockers:**
  - **Soak ≥ 2 h is not yet passed end to end.** It stays open after merge by owner decision ("Merge trước, soak sau"). Every attempt stopped on a harness or operator-network fault; all of them are fixed in `4e3216a` (`evidence/P14f-f6.md`).
  - allow_all above 4,000 on one process is bounded by O(n²) map latency, not by RAM.
  - A registration burst into one tailnet is serialized by the per-tailnet quota lock. The excess is refused with a retryable error. The message `sql: transaction has already been committed or rolled back` is confusing; improving it is a to-do.
  - "fence failed" log lines appear at shutdown: last-seen writes from disconnecting clients after the lease is released are refused, as designed. Last-seen at shutdown is lost (shutdown ordering; P13/P14).
  - Lab PgBouncer (`mc-db`): each F5 step adds and removes a lease alias. `pgbouncer.ini` was verified identical to its backup afterwards.
