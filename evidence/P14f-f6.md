### S08d (exit node) on the P14f binary

S08d.1–S08d.6 and S08d.0 passed in all three N1 runs (ON 1.104.7, ON 1.84.3, OFF 1.84.3). That covers:
- policy and approval through tenant A's API;
- the data path through a2: IPv4 and IPv6, with a2's SNAT visible;
- the `allow-lan-access` behaviour;
- removing `autogroup:internet`, after which the node is no longer offered;
- auto-approval of a tagged exit node;
- B's own exit node: B never reaches INET-A and cannot select A's exit nodes by name or by address;
- `exit-node list` and `exit-node suggest` show only nodes in the client's own tailnet;
- full undo.

The topology analysis and the fix are in `evidence/S08d-exitnode.md`.
